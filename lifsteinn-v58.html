Pick when the fast should end. If a fast is running, its goal shifts to match; if not, one starts now and runs until then.
Extended fasts (24 h+): keep electrolytes up (sodium·potassium·magnesium), and end the fast if you feel dizzy, weak, or wrong — no timer outranks your body. Loop in a doctor for multi-day fasts, especially with PCOS meds. 🧵

🧂 Electrolyte drink "snake juice"

On longer fasts the body loses salt and potassium, which can bring headaches, tiredness, cramps or dizziness. This drink replaces some of what's lost.
Stir into 1 litre of water:
½ tsp salt, table or sea — about 1,150 mg sodium
½ tsp potassium chloride salt substitute (such as NoSalt or Nu-Salt) — about 1,300 mg potassium; check your product's label
¼ tsp baking soda (optional) — about 300 mg more sodium

Sip it slowly across the day rather than drinking it at once. On a fasting day, up to 2 litres is the usual amount.

Magnesium is easier to take as a supplement (citrate or glycinate) than in the drink: follow the label, and keep supplements to 350 mg a day or less.

Today's electrolyte check
Stop and rest if you feel sick, weak, or notice an uneven heartbeat. Talk to a doctor first if you have kidney or heart problems, or take medicine that affects potassium, such as some blood-pressure medicines or spironolactone (often prescribed for PCOS).

🌊 Why weight goes up and down
The scale can swing ±2 kg overnight, and almost none of it is fat. The usual suspects: a salty meal (the body holds water for a day or two) · carbs after a low-carb stretch (every gram of stored glycogen carries 3–4 g of water — this is fuel, not fat) · a late or large meal still in transit · the week before a period (hormonal water, often 1–2 kg) · new or hard exercise (muscles hold water while they repair) · travel, poor sleep, or stress. Actual fat change needs roughly 7,700 kcal per kg — a single day simply can't do that in either direction. The gold line on the chart is the truth; single days are weather. 💙
Measurements often change before weight does.

What is Lífsteinn?

A private companion for your days, named for the life stone everyone carries in the Hverlands.

What it can hold: tasks and a journal · habits and goals · collections of things you keep · food, sleep, cycle and body, if you want them · on a standard calendar or the Hverlands' Moons. Nothing is switched on until you choose it, and anything can be switched off again in ⚙️.

What it does: lives entirely on this device · works offline · exports backups & spreadsheets that are yours to keep · never punishes a missed day.

What it doesn't do: no accounts, no cloud, no ads, no feeds — and no medical advice.
