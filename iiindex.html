📚 Welcome to Myndasögur

A quiet shelf for comics, picture-books, and bound pages — read under the stars.

📚 Add books takes .cbz archives, .pdf files, or a handful of images. Choose several PDFs at once and the merge bench opens — drag rows by the ⠿ grip into order; pages are copied whole (perfect) or pressed toward email size, with filled-in forms pressed flat.

🧵 Bind joins anything already on the shelf, whatever it began as — a PDF with a comic with a bundle of photos. Tap books in order, name the volume, done.

Hold a book (or right-click) for its quick menu — open, rename, download, remove.

In the reader: tap the edges to turn, LTR/RTL for manga, and your place is always remembered.

Books live only in this browser — the originals on your device are never touched.
